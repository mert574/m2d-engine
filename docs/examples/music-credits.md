# Music and sound credits

The demos play tracks from **IDMf001: InDepthMelodics**, the first compilation released by the [IDMf Netlabel](https://archive.org/details/Idmf001IndepthmelodicsfreeElectronicMusicCompilation), released under [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). The recordings are unchanged; the demos lower the volume and loop playback.

| Track | Artist | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stars at Dawn | Nathan Rain | Platformer menu, Beeline menu |
| Perfect Union | The Get | Platformer level, Beeline level |
| Last Transmission | Kloquewurk | Top-down menu, showcase menu, Cinder Keep menu |
| November | Chaotic Pulse | Top-down level, Cinder Keep level |
| Symmetrical Tree | Binary Pulse | Hedgerow Court menu, Salvage Run menu |
| Redux | Halogen | Hedgerow Court level, Salvage Run level |
| G2 (Cockboymattt Remix) | GBSR | Showcase, Ash Row level (its menu plays Last Transmission) |
| Longing for Rain | Evelon | Night Circuit level (its menu plays Stars at Dawn) |

Hollow Grove plays two tracks by [Matthew Pablo](https://www.matthewpablo.com) from OpenGameArt, both [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/): [Woodland Fantasy](https://opengameart.org/content/woodland-fantasy) in the grove and [Enchanted Festival](https://opengameart.org/content/enchanted-festival) on its menu.

The physics playground is sound-effects only. Sound effects are from [Interface Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds) by Kenney, released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The circuit's tyre squeal is by qubodup ([CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/)) from [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org); its electric motor whine is synthesised in code. Hedgerow Court's lawn and hedge and the top-down and Hollow Grove ground textures are [ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com) materials, also CC0; the circuit's sprites are Kenney's [Racing Pack](https://kenney.nl/assets/racing-pack) and Beeline's meadow silhouettes are Kenney's [Foliage Sprites](https://kenney.nl/assets/foliage-sprites), recoloured, both CC0. Hollow Grove's people are Clint Bellanger's [Isometric Hero and Heroine](https://opengameart.org/content/isometric-hero-and-heroine) (CC BY 3.0) and its ability icons are Lorc's from [game-icons.net](https://game-icons.net) (CC BY 3.0); Salvage Run's art and sounds are Kenney's [Space Shooter Redux](https://kenney.nl/assets/space-shooter-redux) (CC0); Ash Row's street and wall are ambientCG materials and its people are Flare's (both as above); Cinder Keep's tiles are Screaming Brain Studios' [Isometric Stone Soup](https://opengameart.org/content/isometric-stone-soup) (CC0) and its skeleton and goblin are from Clint Bellanger's [Isometric Hero and Creatures](https://opengameart.org/content/isometric-hero-and-creatures) (CC BY 3.0); Hollow Grove's trees are Wenrexa's [nature sprites](https://opengameart.org/content/assets-free-nature-sprites-trees-shrubs) and its footsteps are from Owlish Media's [Sound Effects Pack](https://opengameart.org/content/sound-effects-pack), both CC0 on OpenGameArt.

The full notices and license texts are in [`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`](https://github.com/mert574/m2d-engine/blob/master/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) at the repository root.
