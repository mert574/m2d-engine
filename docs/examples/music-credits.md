# Music and sound credits

The demos play seven tracks from **IDMf001: InDepthMelodics**, the first compilation released by the [IDMf Netlabel](https://archive.org/details/Idmf001IndepthmelodicsfreeElectronicMusicCompilation), licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). The recordings are unchanged; the demos lower the volume and loop playback.

| Track | Artist | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stars at Dawn | Nathan Rain | Platformer menu |
| Perfect Union | The Get | Platformer level |
| Last Transmission | Kloquewurk | Top-down menu, showcase menu |
| November | Chaotic Pulse | Top-down level |
| Symmetrical Tree | Binary Pulse | Maze menu |
| Redux | Halogen | Maze level |
| G2 (Cockboymattt Remix) | GBSR | Showcase |

The physics playground is sound-effects only. Sound effects are from [Interface Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds) by Kenney, released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The maze and top-down textures are [ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com) materials, also CC0.

File checksums and the full license texts are in [`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`](https://github.com/mert574/m2d-engine/blob/master/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) at the repository root.
