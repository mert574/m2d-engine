# 3D

Goal: 3D scenes that read as real places, from a library of open materials and models. Roadmap lines: "3D places" and "Asset library".

## Decisions

- Assets and quality follow [[demo-standards]]: free, open assets entering as recipes; the native AAA bar.

## Backlog

**Places:** terrain with height and splat materials; decals and water; curved fences, kerbs and roads along paths; scatter that is aware of orientation; a physical atmosphere, clouds and volumetric fog; falling leaves.

**Asset library:**

- texture fetches that bring the full PBR set;
- models, materials and HDRIs as manifest assets with uids, bound through `uses`;
- `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` rendered from each fetch's `info.json`;
- a starting set of a few dozen materials and a model set per theme.

## Record

[[3d-and-editor]] is the plan the WebGPU renderer grew by. Shipped, Nov 2026: PBR, shadows, clustered lights, IBL, glTF, GPU culling, streaming, post, wind, 3D particles, PBR shape materials, the lot rebuilt as a town corner.
