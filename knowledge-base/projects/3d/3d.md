# 3D

Goal: 3D scenes that read as real places, from a library of open materials and models. Roadmap lines: "3D places" and "Asset library".

## Decisions

- Assets and quality follow [[demo-standards]]: free, open assets entering as recipes; the native AAA bar.
- **Model quality is a project setting, not a compile constant.** A project's `quality` block names texture and model levels as caps over each kind's own sizes; the compiler builds every combination and the settings menu swaps them live (docs: Texture and model quality). The lot ships at the low texture level and the middle model level (512 px, 40,000 triangles), the owner's pick from a side-by-side, which brought the published set from 742 MB to 145 MB. Levels are declared lowest first and named, not numbered, so a settings row steps right into more quality. Pages offers no levels (`offerQuality: false` outside the dev server). (16 Nov 2026.)
- **A file size is measured, not guessed.** The 534 MB of the lot's 746 were textures inside the models and 211 geometry, and the trees were half of both; RDO on UASTC saved a fifth of texture bytes for double the encode time at 2k and next to nothing at 1k, so it stays off.

## Backlog

**Places:** terrain with height and splat materials; decals and water; curved fences, kerbs and roads along paths; scatter that is aware of orientation; a physical atmosphere, clouds and volumetric fog; falling leaves.

**Asset library:**

- texture fetches that bring the full PBR set;
- models, materials and HDRIs as manifest assets with uids, bound through `uses`;
- `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` rendered from each fetch's `info.json`;
- a starting set of a few dozen materials and a model set per theme;
- a conifer built for real time: Poly Haven's `pine_tree_01` is 409,000 needle clusters of seventeen triangles each, so any budget under a few hundred thousand keeps one or two in a hundred and the lot's pines read bare. The compiler no longer breaks them (wood keeps a quarter of level 0, foliage thins to whole cards, a quad never shows another card's texels), but the fix for the look is an asset with twig cards.
