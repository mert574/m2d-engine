M2D Engine Examples

Renderer Showcase

A batching stress test with a live stats overlay: thousands of sprites, GPU particles, dynamic lights and bloom, all counted on screen. Swap to the Canvas backend in the settings menu and compare.

Platformer Game

A simple platformer demonstrating side-scrolling physics, jumping mechanics, and basic character movement.

Top-down Maze

A generated maze explored with a following camera: find the pulsing green goal in the far corner.

Physics Playground

An interactive physics sandbox: a funnel, a splitter and a peg field. Spawn balls and boxes and watch them tumble through.

Top-Down Game

A top-down game demonstrating 8-directional movement, zero gravity physics, and exploration gameplay.

Hollow Grove

A three-quarter view with a small quest: painted trees on footprint colliders, a textured path through wind-blown grass, two people to talk to, three skills, a minimap and a zooming camera.

Cinder Keep

An isometric dungeon: a projection on positions over flat physics, diamond tiles, click-to-move and torchlit block walls that cast shadows.

Ash Row

A belt scroller at high noon: a depth lane compressed by a projection, punch combos and sweep kicks on the engine's attack chains, and a camera that locks per section.

Salvage Run

A rotating top-down space flight: the camera turns with the ship over parallax star fields, meteors split when shot, drones hunt, and twelve pods open the gate.

Night Circuit

Arcade time trials under the lights: drift five circuits, knock barrels into next round's oil, race your ghost and replay your best lap from its recorded inputs.
