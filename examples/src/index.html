M2D Engine Examples

The Yard (3D)

The first three-dimensional scene: primitive shapes, a glTF milk truck and an animated fox under a low sun with soft shadows, a mirror sphere reflecting the sky, glowing lamps, and a dusk to turn on, all on WebGPU. Drag to orbit, WASD to walk. Chromium only.

Renderer Showcase

A batching stress test with a live stats overlay: thousands of sprites, GPU particles, dynamic lights and bloom. Swap backends and compare.

Platformer

Side-scrolling physics, moving platforms, a chasing enemy, coins and a level-complete trigger.

Hedgerow Court

A hedge-maze garden of single-screen courts: the camera glides court to court while daisies bloom along your path.

Beeline

An auto-scroller into the dusk: chain pollen, dash past wasps and out of webs, reach the hive before dark.

Physics Playground

A lamplit machine: a funnel, a splitter and a peg field. Spawn balls and crates, grab and throw them.

Top-Down Game

Eight-directional movement, ghosts that chase, turrets with line of sight and rockets.

Hollow Grove

A three-quarter view of a grove at dusk, with a small quest: footprint colliders, dialogue, skills and a minimap.

Cinder Keep

An isometric dungeon: a projection on positions over flat physics, click-to-move and torchlit walls that cast shadows.

Ash Row

A beat 'em up at high noon: a depth lane compressed by a projection, punch combos and a camera that locks per section.

Salvage Run

Rotating top-down space flight: the view turns with the ship, meteors split when shot, drones hunt, pods open the gate.

Night Circuit

Arcade time trials under the lights: drift, boost, spill oil for the next round, and race your ghost from recorded inputs.
